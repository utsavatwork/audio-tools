MP3 Playground

Privacy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Your files stay on your device

MP3 Playground runs entirely in your web browser. When you open an audio or video file, it is decoded, trimmed and converted on your own device. Your files are never uploaded to our servers or to anyone else, and we cannot see them.

What we collect

We don't use accounts, cookies or advertising trackers. Like any website, our hosting provider (Amazon Web Services) receives standard technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the page to you.

To understand how the site is used, we use Umami, a privacy-friendly analytics service that uses no cookies and doesn't track you across websites. It records anonymous, aggregated information: pages visited, referring site, country, device and browser type, and actions such as "a file was loaded" or "an MP3 was exported", with the file type, whether a part was kept or cut out, whether fades were added, and a rough length (for example "1–5 min"). It never receives your files, their names or their contents.

When you tap "I want this" on an upcoming feature, the vote is counted anonymously the same way. Your browser also remembers which features you voted for (in local storage on your device), so you don't vote twice.

Questions and feedback

Use our feedback form (no account needed). The form is hosted by Tally, a separate service that receives what you enter there. You can leave an email address if you'd like a reply; we only use it to answer you. If you have a GitHub account, you can also open an issue. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information in them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
